Simulate table script summary

Script:
src/simulate_table.py

Inputs:
- data/processed/matches_features.csv
- models/xgboost_model.joblib
- models/preprocessor.joblib

Settings:
- target season: 2025
- simulations: 10000
- points system: win=3, draw=1, loss=0
- ranking tie-breakers: points, goal difference, goals scored, team name

Output files:
- outputs/simulation_summary.csv
- outputs/simulation_position_probabilities.csv

Saved output sizes:
- simulation_summary.csv rows: 96
- simulation_position_probabilities.csv rows: 1848

Summary columns:
- league
- season
- team
- expected_finishing_position
- probability_of_winning_league
- probability_of_finishing_top_4
- probability_of_qualifying_for_europe
- probability_of_relegation
- current_points
- current_goal_difference

Example results:
- Bayern Munich expected position: 1.0001
- Bayern Munich league win probability: 0.9999
- Bayern Munich European qualification probability: 1.0000
- Borussia Dortmund expected position: 2.0010

Notes:
- Probabilities are generated from the encoded and scaled XGBoost input matrix.
- Remaining fixtures are sampled from predicted class probabilities.
- Sampled scorelines are drawn from historical scorelines conditioned on league and outcome.
- Completed matches contribute current points and goal difference before simulation starts.
